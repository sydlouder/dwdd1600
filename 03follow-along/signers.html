Who Were the Signers?

Famous Signers

Some of the most famous signers of the Declaration of Independence include

John Hancock
President of the Continental Congress, famous for his large and distinctive signature.
Benjamin Franklin
Famous scientist, inventor, writer, and diplomat who was one of the most well-known Founding Fathers.
Thomas Jefferson
The principal author of the Declaration of Independence who later became the third President of the United States.
John Adams
A strong supporter of independence who later became the second President of the United States.
Samuel Adams
A revolutionary leader who helped organize resistance against British rule.
John Witherspoon
A minister and educator who was the only active college president to sign the Declaration.
George Walton
A Georgia politician who later served as governor of Georgia.
Roger Sherman
A Connecticut representative and the only person to sign the Continental Association, Declaration of Independence, Articles of Confederation, and U.S. Constitution.
Robert Morris
A wealthy merchant who helped finance the American Revolution and played an important role in the Continental Congress.
Charles Carroll of Carrollton
A wealthy Maryland planter who was the last surviving signer of the Declaration of Independence.

The signers of the Declaration of Independence were important leaders who took a great risk to fight for American independence and helped shape the country we know today.
