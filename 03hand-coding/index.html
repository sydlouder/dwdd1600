Ski the Powder UT

Best Snow on Earth

Utah is home to some of the best skiing and snowboarding in the country, with world-class resorts, breathtaking mountain scenery, and famously light, dry snow. From historic ski areas to modern resorts, Utah offers a wide variety of experiences for beginners and experienced skiers alike.

This website explores the history of skiing in Utah, provides information about the state's major ski resorts, and compares resort pricing to help you understand the differences between them. Whether you are planning your first ski trip or looking to discover a new mountain, this guide is a place to learn more about what Utah's ski resorts have to offer.

Why Ski in Utah?

Utah offers something for every skier, from thrilling terrain to unforgettable mountain views. So, what makes Utah such a great place to ski?

  • Greatest Snow on Earth - Experience Utah's famous light, dry powder.
  • Resorts for Everyone - Choose from a wide variety of ski resorts.
  • Breathtaking Views - Take in Utah's stunning mountain scenery.
  • Terrain for Every Skier - Find slopes ranging from beginner to expert.
  • Easy to Reach - Many resorts are just a short drive from Salt Lake City.
  • A Unique Adventure - Make unforgettable memories on Utah's mountains.

What Kind of Skier Are You?

Every skier has a different idea of the perfect day on the mountain. Which kind of skier are you?

  • Beginner — Find resorts with beginner-friendly terrain and easy slopes.
  • Intermediate — Look for resorts with a good mix of terrain and challenges.
  • Experienced — Discover challenging runs, steep terrain, and bigger thrills.
  • Powder Chaser — Search for Utah's famous deep, fresh powder.
  • Budget Traveler — Compare resorts to find options that fit your budget.

Finding out what kind of skier you are can help you discover which resorts and mountain areas in Utah best match your experience, interests, and the kind of adventure you're looking for!
