Digital Citizenship

What is Digital Citizenship?

Digital citizenship is the responsible, safe, and ethical use of digital technologies such as the internet, social media, computers, and mobile devices.

What is Digital Law?

Digital law refers to the laws and rules that govern how people use technology, computers, the internet, and digital information.

What is AUP?

It is a set of rules that explain how people are allowed to use a school's, company's, or organization's technology and internet.

What is Plagiarism

Plagiarism is when you use someone else's words, ideas, images, or work and present them as your own without giving them credit.

In simple terms: Plagiarism is taking someone else's work or ideas and claiming them as your own

What is Academic Dishonesty?

Academic dishonesty is when a student cheats, lies, or uses unfair methods to gain an advantage in schoolwork or exams.

In simple terms: Academic dishonesty means being dishonest or unfair in your schoolwork to get a better grade or advantage.

What are the rules for AI Generated Content?

The rules for AI-generated content depend on the school, teacher, website, or organization.

You should use AI responsibly, follow the rules for the assignment, be honest about your use of it, and make sure the final work meets your teacher's requirements.
